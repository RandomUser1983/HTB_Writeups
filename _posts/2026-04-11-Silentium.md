## Review
Solid easy machine with a fun and logical chain.
Enumeration is key to find the foothold, which involves a known vulnerability in a self-hosted web service. From there, some lateral 
thinking is needed to escape to the host. 
The privilege escalation is creative and involves a lesser-known vulnerability that was new to me.


**Personal Rating:** ⭐⭐⭐ (3.5/5)

**Recommended for:** Those who enjoy web enumeration and chaining real-world vulnerabilities.
