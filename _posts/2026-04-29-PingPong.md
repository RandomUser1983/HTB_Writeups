## Review
This was actually the first time I completed an Insane active machine. Lot of things to say, but I will limit myself to avoid spoilers. 
I loved the concept of the machine, PingPong is for sure the right name. 
I also liked the fact that the entire environment is pretty close to what you could find in the real world, where less secure protocols are disabled by default.
I had to adjust my usual attack methodology to work around their absence, and that's what I liked the most about it. Great job building this one.

**Personal Rating:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4.5/5)

**Recommended for:**  Those who are ready for a challenge and don't mind losing hair and sleep.
