## Review
This was the best machine so far, not only because is the first hard one, but because of the flow of it.
A lot of different vulnerabilities to chain, not one of them was based on a public CVE, just pure AD.
Of course since it's an hard machine there are a few rabbit holes to pay attention to.


**Personal Rating:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4.5/5)

**Recommended for:** People who already understand very well how AD works, but want to practice some less known vulnerabilities
